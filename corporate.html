Event Photography

Corporate Event Photography

Corporate event photography is crucial for capturing the essence of business gatherings, including the atmosphere, attendees, and key moments.

We collaborate with event planners to understand the event's nature and objectives, helping determine the need for multiple photographers.

Corporate event photography includes group photos, headshots, and candid shots of attendees networking and interacting.

Social Event Photography

Social event photography is an important aspect of any business event. It captures the feel of the event, the attendees, the speakers, and the overall atmosphere.

We work together with the planning of the corporate event so we can understand the type of event it is, the audience, and the desired outcome of the photographs. During the planning process, this will help us determine the number of photographers that will be needed to make sure there is full coverage of the important details

When do you need more than 1 photographer to capture your corporate event? If there is a large conference with more than 5,000 attendees, multiple photographers are highly recommended. This will help to ensure that the different speakers and sessions are documented in parallel.

What type of photography is considered corporate event photography? It can include Group Photos, Headshots, and candid photographs of Attendees Networking and Interacting.
